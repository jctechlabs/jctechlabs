# JC TechLabs

**Enterprise AI automation, custom software engineering and connected growth systems.**

We help new and established businesses connect their workflows, software, acquisition and customer operations. Delivery starts with an operating problem, clear data ownership and testable acceptance criteria.

[Website](https://jctechlabs.com/) · [Interactive work](https://jctechlabs.com/work/) · [Industry solutions](https://jctechlabs.com/industries/) · [Contact](https://jctechlabs.com/contact/)

## Core capabilities

- [AI and automation](https://jctechlabs.com/services/ai-automation/): AI integrations, assistants, workflow automation and human-review handoffs.
- [Custom software engineering](https://jctechlabs.com/services/software/): web applications, SaaS, mobile applications, internal systems and API integrations.
- [Digital marketing and growth systems](https://jctechlabs.com/services/digital-growth/): acquisition measurement, landing pages, conversion funnels and marketing automation.
- [Google review management](https://jctechlabs.com/services/google-reviews/): monitoring, response workflows and policy-based reporting. Removal decisions remain with Google; negative sentiment alone is not a removal basis.

## Explore the working models

- [Automation capacity planner](https://jctechlabs.com/tools/automation-planner/): model task volumes, staff handling, human review and maintenance. Four sample workflows include support triage and appointment routing.
- [Google review rating and velocity calculator](https://jctechlabs.com/tools/google-review-calculator/): compare targets using exact star counts or ranges from a rounded rating.
- [Software scope and architecture estimator](https://jctechlabs.com/tools/software-scope-estimator/): inspect first-release scope assumptions, interfaces and operating controls.
- [Lead funnel, response-time and ROAS calculator](https://jctechlabs.com/tools/lead-funnel-calculator/): connect acquisition inputs to expected sales, contribution and response-time gaps.
- [AI automation ROI calculator](https://jctechlabs.com/tools/ai-automation-roi-calculator/): distinguish capacity value from realized cash benefit.
- [Integration recovery sandbox](https://jctechlabs.com/work/integration-recovery/): follow a synthetic event through a failed handoff, retry and duplicate replay.
